Welcome to Ternt

A browser and a terminal in one window. Spaces on the left, the web and your shell on the right.

Start here

⌘T
Command bar. Type a URL, a search, a tab name, or > cmd to run a command in a new terminal.
⌥⌘T
New terminal tab. Your shell, right next to your tabs.
⌘L
Edit the current URL.
⌘S
Hide or show the sidebar.
⌘F
Explore tabs. Move with the arrow keys, Space peeks, Enter opens.
⌃1–9
Switch spaces. Or swipe two fingers across the sidebar.

The sidebar

Favorites

Icon tiles at the top. Drag a tab there to keep it in every space that shares the profile.

Pinned

Per space, in folders if you like. Closing a pinned tab unloads it and keeps it in the list.

Today

Everything else, newest first. Hover the divider for Clear.

Spaces & profiles

Each new space gets its own cookie jar, so logins stay where you put them. Right-click a space dot to share one.

Press ⌘T to open your first tab. Ternt updates itself: an Update button appears in the sidebar when a new version is out.

Shortcuts, architecture and source: github.com/jonkarrer/ternt. This page is always one click away under Help → Welcome to Ternt.
